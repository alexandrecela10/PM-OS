Alexandre is interviewing for a PM role at Orbitron AI (makers of NovaOS, FlowNova, ComplyNova). ## What we learned

### About Orbitron
- Asset Nova already exists on their website footer as a named product (fourth in the NovaOS lineup). This is not an invention exercise — it's proposing how to build something they've committed to.
- NovaOS is a composable agent studio (NovaStudio): visual workflow canvas, reusable agents as drag-in nodes, "Manager Review" (human gate) is a first-class step, ERP Sync built in.
- Founder Ashu Gupta's LinkedIn posts reveal the hidden grading rubric:
  - Anti-SaaS: "Dashboards decay. Skills compound." No static dashboards — agents render throwaway views on demand.
  - Governance is the product: every agent needs an owner, defined scope + escalation, value metric (not activity).
  - Sovereign AI / "Keep your alpha": for regulated industries (KSA), customer data stays local, no wrapper plays.
- FlowNova covers: command center, business planning, contracting & procurement, operations, logistics.
- ComplyNova covers: third-party risk, case command center, internal audit automation.
- GTM is outcome-based pricing ("ROI guaranteed"), KSA-first (IKTVA, SACS-002 compliance).

### About the CPM industry
- Discovery report ingested (`cpm-agentic-ai-product-discovery.md`): 45K words, 48 cited sources covering lifecycle (FEL 1-3 → FID → EPC → commissioning → O&M), org charts, tool landscape, 7 ranked pain points, structural analysis, 6 agentic opportunities, buying reality.
- Headline stats: 89% of Middle East megaprojects over cost, 87% late. Average O&G overrun = 59% vs FID. 33% of capex disputed (HKA CRUX). Average ME dispute = $91M, 12.5 months.
- Gap analysis: all AI competitors are construction-phase, single-silo, contractor-side tools. Nobody serves the owner PMO reading across P6 + SAP + Aconex on process-industry megaprojects. Nobody touches FEL (where outcomes are decided).
- Buying reality: 9-18+ month cycles, vendor registration gates, IKTVA/ICV localization scoring, SACS-002 cybersecurity cert, data residency requirements, paid pilot on 1-2 projects before enterprise deal.

### Key decisions made
1. **Wedge product = Commercial Guardian** (claims/notices agent), not budget estimation or compliance DD. Reason: Hormozi value equation — dollar-denominated dream outcome, provable via evidence chains, days-to-value, minimal deployment effort.
2. **3-horizon roadmap:** H1 = Commercial Guardian + Schedule Interrogator (land), H2 = EAC Reconciler + Deliverable Chaser (expand), H3 = FID Red Team + Handover Assembler (differentiate).
3. **Prototype approach:** single unified HTML demo showing a $42.3M exposure found in project correspondence, with evidence chain, notice draft, human approval, and NovaStudio-style orchestration across ComplyNova + FlowNova + Schedule Interrogator.
4. **Deck structure (updated per user feedback):** demo/prototype FIRST (the "what"), then the "how." From X → To Y transformation framing. Inspired by user's liquidity deck structure.
