## GC 4.12 — Unforeseeable Physical Conditions

(a) If the Contractor encounters physical conditions which it considers to have
been Unforeseeable, meaning not reasonably foreseeable by an experienced
contractor by reference to the Geotechnical Baseline Report, the Contractor
shall give notice to the Engineer as soon as practicable, describing the
conditions and the reasons they are considered Unforeseeable.

(b) **Formal notice with particulars shall be given no later than 28 days after
the Contractor became aware, or should have become aware, of the conditions.**
The notice shall include: (i) the location and nature of the conditions;
(ii) contemporaneous records evidencing the conditions; (iii) an initial
estimate of the effect on the Time for Completion and the Contract Price.

(c) Failure to give notice within the period in (b) shall constitute a waiver
of any entitlement to an extension of time or additional payment arising from
such conditions. **[This is the time bar the agent tracks.]**

## GC 20.1 — Owner's Claims

Where the Owner considers itself entitled to any payment or reduction, the
Owner shall give notice and particulars to the Contractor no later than 28 days
after the Owner became aware of the event or circumstance.
