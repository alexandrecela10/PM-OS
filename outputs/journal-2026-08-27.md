## What changed in the thinking since round 1

Commercial Guardian is now named for what it does: claims defence and recovery. It is designed as an always-on watcher rather than a workspace, because the trigger is "the record changed", not "a person opened the tool". Every ROI-sized item goes through a human review gate, and the agent drafts but never sends.

Adoption is now treated as the product decision rather than a go-to-market detail. Six rules: read-only files, no new habit, the agent never sends, every claim is clickable, proof on closed projects first, priced against one avoided loss.
